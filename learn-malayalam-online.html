FAQ

Malayalam Class Questions

Do we need to already speak Malayalam at home?

No — we teach complete beginners as well as children who understand some spoken Malayalam but haven't learned to read or write it.

Are the teachers native Malayalam speakers?

Yes. Our Malayalam teachers are native speakers from Kerala, so your child learns authentic pronunciation and cultural context, not just vocabulary.

Can adults join too?

Yes. We welcome learners of all ages, from young children (3+) to adults, including parents learning alongside their kids.

Is this only for families in Kerala or India?

Not at all — most of our Malayalam students are diaspora families spread across 21+ countries who want their children to stay connected to Kerala's language and culture, no matter where they live.
