FAQ

Hindi Class Questions

Does my child need to speak Hindi at home already?

No — many of our students come from homes where Hindi isn't spoken day-to-day. Classes start from wherever your child actually is, including complete beginners.

Can adults join the Hindi classes too?

Yes. We teach learners of all ages, from young children (3+) to adults.

What does a Hindi class actually cover?

Depending on level, classes cover Devanagari script reading and writing, conversational speaking from day one, storytelling and role-play, and Indian culture and festivals.

We live in India but Hindi still isn't easy to find locally — can you help?

Yes. A number of our Hindi students are actually based in India, in states where formal Hindi instruction isn't easily available. Our teachers work with students both in India and across the diaspora.
