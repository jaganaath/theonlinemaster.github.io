FAQ

Maths Tuition Questions

Which curriculum boards do you support?

We support CBSE, ICSE, IB, Australian, UK and US curriculum boards for Maths tuition.

Is this rote learning or exam drilling?

Neither — our approach is concept-first. We focus on building real number sense and problem-solving confidence, using practice worksheets and progress tracking to support whatever board your child follows.

What age range do you teach?

We welcome learners of all ages, from young children (3+) through secondary school, with content matched to your child's level and board.

Can my child take Maths alongside a language class?

Yes — many families combine Maths tuition with one of our language courses (Tamil, Hindi or Malayalam), scheduled around the same time zone convenience.
