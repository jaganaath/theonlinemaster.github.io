FAQ

Crochet Class Questions

Do I need any experience to join?

No prior experience needed — we start from the very basics and build up step by step.

What materials do I need?

Just simple, affordable crochet hooks and yarn to get started — your teacher will guide you on exactly what to pick up before your first class.

Can parents and kids join the same session?

Yes — parent-and-child sessions are a popular option, and many families find it's a relaxing activity to do together.

Is this just for kids, or can adults join too?

Both. We teach kids and adults, from complete beginners upward, in 1:1 or small group sessions.
